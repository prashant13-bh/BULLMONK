1. The BullMonk Privacy Pledge

At BullMonk, data sovereignty is not an afterthought; it is our foundation. As a publisher investigating geopolitical and financial systems, we respect the absolute privacy of our readers, subscribers, and sources. We will never sell, lease, or broker your personal information to third-party ad exchanges or data brokers.

2. Information We Collect

We restrict data collection to the minimum technical footprint necessary to deliver our services:

  • Subscriber Contact Data: Email addresses provided voluntarily for "The Morning Monk" newsletter or institutional dispatches.
  • Local Storage Preferences: Your saved reading lists, night/day theme preferences, and locally authored draft articles are stored client-side in your own browser's LocalStorage.
  • Anonymous Technical Telemetry: Aggregated page view counts and latency benchmarks stripped of IP addresses and device identifiers.

3. Whistleblower & Anonymous Source Protection

Communications sent via our Secure Whistleblower Tip Line are processed through zero-knowledge encrypted channels. BullMonk systems do not log source IP addresses, browser user-agents, or session timestamps for investigative tips. Our journalists adhere strictly to the International Federation of Journalists' Source Protection Doctrine.

4. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

You retain the right to request full disclosure of any data associated with your subscription, request instantaneous erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"), or export your reading dossier in portable JSON format. Contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@bullmonk.intelligence.

BullMonk Editorial Code of Honor

1. Absolute Editorial Independence

BullMonk operates completely independent of commercial sponsors, sovereign wealth funds, political parties, or financial institutions. No advertiser, shareholder, or parent entity exercises pre-publication editorial review or veto power over our investigative reports.

2. Verification & The Two-Source Standard

Factual claims and leaked documents published as investigative scoops must undergo independent corroboration by at least two distinct, verified sources or verified primary cryptographic/documentary evidence. Where official responses cannot be obtained before publication deadline, reasonable notice is always extended to all named parties.

3. Financial Conflicts of Interest

BullMonk financial journalists and macro analysts are strictly barred from trading equities, sovereign debt, derivatives, or tokens within seventy-two (72) hours of authoring or editing reports regarding those specific instruments or industries. All personal holdings exceeding $10,000 in covered entities must be disclosed to the Bureau Standards Editor.

4. Artificial Intelligence & Attribution

All articles published on BullMonk are researched, drafted, and verified by human investigative journalists. While algorithmic tools may assist with econometric modeling or computational synthesis of public registries, every sentence is vetted and signed by an accredited editor. Synthetic voice narrations are clearly labeled as artificial simulations.

Transparent Accountability

1. The BullMonk Errata Protocol

Trust is fragile and non-negotiable. When factual, quantitative, or typographical errors occur in our reporting, BullMonk corrects them promptly and with full transparency.

  • Substantive Corrections: Any alteration to fact, quote, or statistical figure is prominently noted at the foot of the dispatch, detailing the exact previous text, the correction, and the timestamp.
  • Clarity Notes: Clarifications that provide additional context without altering the underlying factual accuracy are labeled as an "Editorial Note".
  • Retraction Standards: In the rare event that an article's foundational premise is proven unsound, the article will be formally retracted with a signed explanation by the Editor-in-Chief.

To notify our Standards Desk of an error, email corrections@bullmonk.intelligence.

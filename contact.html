Frequently Answered Protocols

INQUIRY FAQ
How can an outside analyst or journalist publish articles on BullMonk?

We offer our live browser-based BullMonk Editorial Studio, which allows contributors to compose stories with rich typography, assign bureau tags, choose high-resolution editorial imagery, and publish directly to the global front. For syndication across our institutional terminal feeds, submissions are evaluated by our Zurich standards desk within 48 hours.

How does BullMonk ensure whistleblower tips remain confidential?

Our Tip Line infrastructure does not retain web server logs, IP addresses, or browser metadata. Files submitted through our portal are stored in zero-knowledge encrypted buckets accessible only by authorized investigative editors with physical YubiKey hardware tokens.

Can academic institutions or macro funds syndicate BullMonk data?

Yes. Non-profit academic institutions may cite our findings with canonical hyperlinking. Quantitative hedge funds and corporate treasuries may apply for our structured API feed license through syndicate@bullmonk.intelligence.
